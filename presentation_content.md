# Presentation Content: DeepTrace - Multimodal Deepfake Detection

---

## Slide 1: Abstract
**Title:** Multimodal Deepfake Detection: Combining Spatial, Frequency, and Temporal Cues
*   **Overview:** DeepTrace is a state-of-the-art deepfake detection system designed to identify manipulation in face images and videos.
*   **Core Approach:** Utilizing a multimodal architecture, it analyzes multiple aspects of potential forgery simultaneously.
*   **Key Components:** Integrates spatial anomalies (RGB), frequency artifacts (DCT), temporal inconsistencies (Video Swin), physiological unnaturalness, and CLIP-aligned feature matching.
*   **Current Status:** V1 is fully implemented and trained on image data, achieving 90% accuracy and an AUC of 0.9496 on test data.
*   **Future Scope:** Extensible V2 architecture already researched for multi-spectral analysis, identity consistency, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

## Slide 2: Introduction
**Title:** Introduction to DeepTrace
*   **What is it?** A machine learning pipeline designed to robustly detect synthetic manipulations in facial imagery.
*   **The Problem:** Modern deepfakes generated by GANs and Diffusion models are becoming visually indistinguishable from real media.
*   **Our Solution:** Moving beyond simple image classification by analyzing the *multi-dimensional fingerprints* left by generative processes.
*   **Project Scope:** Currently capable of high-accuracy image analysis, with the architectural foundation laid for comprehensive temporal video analysis.

---

## Slide 3: Motivation
**Title:** The Need for Robust Detection
*   **Visual Realism:** Generative AI tools (GANs, SD, Midjourney) produce outputs that easily fool human eyes and traditional spatial detectors.
*   **The "Arms Race":** As generators improve, detectors that only look at RGB pixels are easily bypassed.
*   **Beyond the Visible:** Forgeries often contain hidden errors in how they compress data, how colors shift over time, or how facial features align with known biological norms.
*   **Goal:** Build a detector that looks at *how* an image was constructed across multiple domains, not just what it looks like.

## Slide 5: Literature Survey
**Title:** Foundations & Related Work
*   **Backbone & Scaling:** *EfficientNet: Rethinking Model Scaling* supports our choice of EfficientNet-B0 for efficient spatial/frequency feature extraction.
*   **Frequency Domain Analysis:** *Spatial-Phase Shallow Learning* justifies our use of explicit Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) processing to expose compression and phase artifacts.
*   **Temporal & Biological Signals:** *FakeCatcher* outlines how biological signals (PPG) expose fakes, informing our physiological encoder. *Video Swin Transformer* informs our temporal modeling backbone.
*   **Generalization via Vision-Language:** *CLIPping the Deception* supports using frozen CLIP encoders to align detector features against a broader, unseen knowledge base.
*   **Calibration:** *On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks* guides our implementation of temperature scaling for reliable confidence scores.

## Slide 6: Research Gap
**Title:** Identifying the Gaps
*   **Single-Modality Weakness:** Most open-source deepfake detectors rely solely on spatial (RGB CNN) analysis, which fails against high-quality modern generators.
*   **Lack of Frequency Focus:** Compression and upsampling artifacts are often ignored despite being reliable indicators of synthetic media.
*   **Poor Generalization:** Detectors trained on one dataset (e.g., FaceForensics++) often fail completely on another (e.g., Celeb-DF) or against novel Diffusion models.
*   **Missing Explainability:** Many models act as black boxes, providing a score without localizing *where* the manipulation occurred.

## Slide 8: Proposed Methodology
**Title:** Multimodal Architecture
*   Our current V1 architecture (DeepfakeDetector) is a synthesized pipeline:
*   **1. Spatial Encoder:** Pretrained EfficientNet-B0 analyzes RGB face crops for blending/texture inconsistencies.
*   **2. Frequency Encoder:** A second EfficientNet-B0 processes 8x8 block DCT (Discrete Cosine Transform) representations to expose compression artifacts.
*   **3. CLIP Alignment:** Aligns the spatial features with a frozen CLIP ViT-B/32 encoder to improve robustness against unseen generators.
*   **4. Multimodal Fusion:** Cross-attention mechanisms fuse these distinct signals so spatial and frequency layers inform one another.
*   **5. Detection & Calibration:** A multi-task head predicts real/fake probabilities and manipulation types, augmented by automated temperature scaling for precise thresholding.

## Slide 9: Proposed Methodology Context 
**Title:** The V1 Current State & Models
*   **Current Deployment:** Actively utilizing the CNN-based (EfficientNet) spatial and frequency modalities for single-image inference. 
*   **Training Snapshot:** Tested successfully on Kaggle Real-vs-Fake datasets.
*   **Explainability:** Employs GradCAM heatmaps to visually localize manipulated regions.
*   **Architecture Status:** Built to support video (Temporal and Physiological modules exist in code), but currently executing and validated on the image-centric flow.

## Slide 10: Conclusion & Future Work
**Title:** Looking Ahead
*   **Conclusion:** We have successfully built and evaluated a highly accurate (90%) image-based deepfake detector that fuses spatial and multi-frequency signals efficiently.
*   **Future Work - Temporal Activation:** Fully train and activate the Video Swin Transformer and BiLSTM physiological branches for robust video sequence detection.
*   **Future Work - RAG Integration:** Implement the researched Retrieval-Augmented Generation (FAISS-based) module to compare active forensic signatures against a known database of manipulation artifacts.
*   **Future Work - Multi-Spectral Focus:** Migrate to the V2 architecture incorporating advanced FFT, wavelets, and SRM noise residuals.
